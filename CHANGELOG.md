# Changelog

What changed in each version of Coco. Downloads are on the [releases page](https://github.com/ClipXonchain/coco-releases/releases).

## 0.1.6 · 3 Nov 2026

- **Open Coco at login.** A new switch in Settings › General › Startup, so Coco is back in the notch and menu bar after a restart. Coco has to be in your Applications folder for it to work.
- **Required update.** Coco 0.1.3 to 0.1.5 ask you to download 0.1.6.

## 0.1.5 · 3 Nov 2026

- **The Binance tab is now Markets.**
- **Premium follows as many X accounts as you like** from the Voices list. A trial still follows two.
- **Pulse is the default alert sound.** If you already picked a sound, it stays.

## 0.1.4 · 2 Nov 2026

- **X: pick your voices.** Market voices became a **Voices** list on the X tab, with a search box. Show an account's latest posts without alerts, follow it for alerts, and remove or restore any of the built-in accounts.
- **Alert rules follow their section.** A category follows its section ("same as section") until you give it a different level. Saved rules that only repeated the section's level are tidied on launch.
- **SurfAi is now Briefs.**
- **Live News** rows no longer show the same Binance News logo on every row, and article text leaves out Square's footer and reply count.
- **New alert sounds:** Crystal, Nova and Pulse.

## 0.1.3 · 29 Oct 2026

- **News and X come through Coco's own server**, pushed live instead of polled. X is its own tab with its own alert rules.
- **Coco Premium** (card and sound alerts, X tracking). Every Mac gets a **3-day trial**. Already have a key? Settings › General › Coco Premium › Activate.
- **Alert rules per category:** each announcement type, news topic and followed X account can have its own level, on the island and in Settings › Notifications.
- **Market voices** skips off-topic posts, and old items that a feed resends no longer alert.
- **Settings** redesigned, with a Plan row and the full notification controls.
- The old optional X token setting is gone: X no longer needs one.
